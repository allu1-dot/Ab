# Hero Lock — the single source of truth

Every asset in this repo is generated against this block. If a generated asset
disagrees with anything here, the asset is wrong, not this file.

## Palette

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Deep navy | `#1B3A6B` | Environment, bounce, gradients, shadow tint |
| Signal orange | `#C4551A` | Accent only — dial, seal ring, single highlight |
| Near-black | `#0A0F1A` | Falloff, void backgrounds behind cutout subjects |
| Cold white | `#E8F1F7` | Specular hits, condensation, ice |

Signal orange is an **accent**, never a field. It appears on the dial and
nowhere else unless the asset spec says otherwise.

## Lighting

Cold gym-fridge key light. Single hard-ish key from **upper left at 5600K**,
raking across the bottle to pick out the ribs. Narrow navy bounce card at
right lifts the shadow side to `#1B3A6B` without filling it. Deep falloff to
near-black — the background never goes grey. Fine condensation beading on
chilled glass, a few droplets tracking down the shoulder.

## Camera (invariant across the flavour series)

100 mm macro, f/5.6, camera locked at bottle-label height, 1.2 m subject
distance, three-quarter front view, bottle centred, dead-level horizon, no
tilt. Macro depth of field: label plane sharp, background fully dissolved.

## Product

Clear rPET bottle. Seven fine horizontal ribs. Conical shoulder. 38 mm collar.
Moulded two-ring vortex base. Flavour dial at the collar. Label zone on the
left face stays **clean and empty** — packaging art drops in later.

## Global negatives

No text. No logo. No lettering of any kind. No hands (the video allows exactly
one clean twist). No stylised, cartoon, or illustrated rendering. No warm
domestic light. No grey background. No orange spill outside the dial.
